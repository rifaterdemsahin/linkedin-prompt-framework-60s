🗺️ Preproduction plan

🎯 One setup, one lens, three full takes. No B-roll day. Graphics are added in the edit.

🎯 1. Outcome

A 60-second LinkedIn native video that stops the scroll with a claim, teaches a 4-part prompting framework, and sends people to the Delivery Pilot Skool community.

Primary export: 1080×1350 (4:5) · Safety export: 1080×1080 (1:1)

📱 2. Audience & platform

LinkedIn feed, mobile-first, sound off. Career-minded operators who already dabble in ChatGPT and still get mid answers. They will not click a YouTube link. They will tap a first comment if the hook earned it.

🎥 3. Single shot

  • 📷 Medium close-up, eyes on the lens, chest-up.
  • 🔒 Camera locked on sticks. No handheld. No zoom.
  • 📺 Elgato Prompter in front of the lens. iPad only as backup, 5–15 cm under the lens.
  • 🟩 Green screen fills the frame with 30–50 cm of spill margin around you.

✨ 4. On-screen graphics (edit only)

  • 🎣 0:00 burned-in hook caption.
  • 🧱 0:25–0:42 four lower-thirds, one beat each: 🎭 ROLE / 📖 CONTEXT / 🚧 CONSTRAINTS / ✅ TASK.
  • 🏁 0:50 end card: Delivery Pilot · skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938
  • 🔇 Captions the whole way. 80%+ of LinkedIn video is watched muted.

🧰 5. Kit

  • 📱 Phone or camera, 4K 30fps, 24–35mm equivalent, f/2.8–4 so the key stays sharp.
  • 💡 Key + fill + hair light. Hair light is mandatory with a beard (despill).
  • 🎤 Lav or shotgun 20–30 cm off-axis. Record 48 kHz.
  • 📺 Elgato Prompter in Camera Hub Text mode. Copy the script from this page (blank lines = chapters).
  • 🖥️ This page as a backup web prompter. Phone as a slate/timer.

👔 6. Wardrobe & talent

  • 🚫 No green, no lime, no fine stripes, no shiny logos.
  • 🟦 Navy, charcoal, white, or rust. LinkedIn reads “operator,” not “YouTuber.”
  • ✨ Matte skin, beard trimmed, no dangling earrings that the key will chew.
  • 🧍 Stand, don’t sit — energy matches the script.

🧪 Sanity check

      
✅ The script is shootable at ~60 seconds. ⚠️ Three copy issues to decide before you record, not after.

      

        

          
            
🔍 Check🏁 Result🎥 What to do on set

          
          
            

              ⏱️ Duration vs word count
              ✅ PASS · 147 words
              At 145–155 wpm this lands at 57–61s. Do not race the hook.
            

            

              🎣 Hook density 0:00–0:10
              ⚠️ TIGHT · 35 words / 10s
              That is ~210 wpm if you force the clock. Speak it in 12s, or drop “next-generation” once. Do not clip the first sentence — that is the feed hook.
            

            

              🔁 “Master this loop”
              ⚠️ AMBIGUOUS
              The last thing you said was a 4-box framework, not a loop. The loop lived in the Shift beat. Either say “Master this framework” or add “Then you iterate.” Use the patched take in the prompter if you want it locked.
            

            

              🎓 “AI Certified”
              ⚠️ UNDEFINED
              LinkedIn viewers will ask “which cert?” On-screen end card: Delivery Pilot · AI Certified. Spoken option: “Get AI Certified with Delivery Pilot.”
            

            

              🏫 “Our course / our Skool”
              ⚠️ UNNAMED
              Name it once. Delivery Pilot is the community. First comment carries https://www.skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938/
            

            

              💬 “Link is in the comments”
              ✅ OK FOR LINKEDIN
              Native video has no in-video hyperlink. Put the Skool URL in the post body and again as the first comment within 30 seconds of publish. Say “first comment,” not “comments,” if you use the patched take.
            

            

              🧱 Four-item list
              ✅ WORKS IF YOU PAUSE
              Count them on camera (1–2–3–4) and hold 0.4s after each word so ROLE / CONTEXT / CONSTRAINTS / TASK lower-thirds can land. Without the pause the list is noise.
            

            

              💼 Tone vs LinkedIn
              ✅ PASS
              Energetic and direct is correct. Do not smile-through the hook; land the claim, then open up on the CTA.
            

            

              🟩 Green screen + LinkedIn codec
              ⚠️ EDGE RISK
              LinkedIn recompresses hard. A dirty key turns into a green halo. Hair light + 1.5–2 m off the screen + despill in Resolve is not optional.
            

            

              🔇 Muted playback
              🛑 MUST FIX IN EDIT
              If captions are missing, most of the audience never hears the framework. Burn in large captions. Hook line on-screen in the first 2 seconds.

🟩 Green screen in the shot

      
🛠️ The green is a production tool, not the LinkedIn look. Viewers should never see chroma. They should see you on a dark navy studio wall.

      
🟩 CHROMA · #00B140 · EVEN · WRINKLE-FREE

      

        

          
💡 Light the screen separately from you

          
    
            
  1. 🧹 Stretch the screen. Wrinkles become dark blobs that will not key.
    2. 
            
  2. 💡 Two LED panels on the screen only, even green, no hot spots. Meter it — it should look boring.
    3. 
            
  3. 📏 Stand 1.5–2 metres off the screen. Closer = spill in the beard and ears.
    4. 
            
  4. 🔦 Key light 45° off-axis. Fill opposite, one stop down.
    5. 
            
  5. ✨ Hair / rim light from behind-above. This is what saves the beard on LinkedIn compression.
    6. 
            
  6. 🚫 No green wardrobe, no reflective watch face pointed at the screen.
    7. 
          

        

        

          
✅ What we check before “action”

          
    
            
  • 🟩 Green fills the entire 4:5 crop, including above the head and past both shoulders.
    • 
            
  • ✂️ No floor spill in the 4:5 crop — or we crop it out in the edit.
    • 
            
  • 📺 Prompter brightness low enough that it does not bounce green into the eyes.
    • 
            
  • 👏 A 5-second clap + “take 1” slate at the head of every take.
    • 
            
  • 🤫 One 10-second room-tone hold after the last take.
    • 
            
  • 🔒 Do not change position between takes. The key track depends on it.
    • 
          

        

      

    


    

      
🖼️ LinkedIn background (the plate you key onto)

      

        LinkedIn is a workplace feed. The background has to read as a dark executive studio, not a fake city, not a gamer wall, not a stock office with a plant. Navy + a thin LinkedIn-blue floor wash. Empty centre so your face is the only subject.
      

      

        

          
🎨 Why this plate

          
    
            
  • Colour: charcoal-navy, close to LinkedIn’s own dark UI. The blue LED is an accent, not a neon club.
    • 
            
  • Texture: large panels, no busy pattern. Compression will wreck fine detail.
    • 
            
  • Depth: a little floor so you are not a floating head. Keep the LED below the 4:5 safe crop if it fights the captions.
    • 
            
  • No logos on the plate. Brand lives in the lower-third and the end card, where we control the type.
    • 
          

        

        

          
📐 Framing on LinkedIn

          
    
            
  • Hero export 4:5 (1080×1350). Fills the mobile feed without going full-screen immersive, so the post text stays visible.
    • 
            
  • Safety 1:1 (1080×1080) if you also post from desktop.
    • 
            
  • Keep eyes in the upper third. Leave 8–10% padding on the right — LinkedIn’s reaction column sits there on 9:16. 4:5 is safer.
    • 
            
  • Do not use 16:9 as the LinkedIn master. Landscape shrinks in the phone feed. Keep 16:9 only as a YouTube/Skool extra.
